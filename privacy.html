01Overview

Havën Schedule (“Havën”, “we”, “us”) is a personal productivity web application that offers a time-blocking calendar, activity tracking, analytics, goals, finance tools and optional AI assistance. This Privacy Policy explains what information the service processes, why, and the choices you have.

The short version: your content is yours. Schedules, tasks, notes and logs you create are stored to provide the service and are not sold or used for advertising. Guest mode works without any personal data at all.

02Data We Collect

Information you provide when signing in

  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google name, email address and profile picture from Google’s authentication service.
  • Email sign-in: if you create an account with an email and password, we store your email address and a secure, salted hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Guest mode: guest sessions do not require a name, email address or any other personal information.

Content you create

  • Schedule tasks, categories and subcategories, goals, activity completions, sleep logs, Pomodoro records, finance entries, gallery items and preferences you enter into the app.
  • Profile details you optionally add, such as a display name, pronouns, occupation, routines or preferences used to personalize AI responses.
  • Messages you send to the built-in AI assistant, if you use it.

Automatically processed technical data

  • Last-seen timestamps that keep synced devices up to date.
  • Standard, short-lived request and security logs operated by our hosting and authentication providers.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, or browsing history from other sites.

03How We Use Data

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in across sessions and devices.
  • To store and sync your content so it appears on the devices you sign in from.
  • To provide app features such as analytics, streaks, exports and reminders of your own data back to you.
  • To respond to AI requests you initiate, when an API key is configured.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse and diagnose technical problems.

We do not use your data to train machine-learning models, and we do not sell or share your personal information with data brokers or advertisers.

04Storage & Security

Your data is stored in two places:

  • On your device: most app data lives locally in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB) so the app works even offline.
  • In the cloud: when you sign in, your content is synced to Google Firebase Firestore under our Firebase project to make it available across your devices. Firebase stores it in the Google Cloud region(s) configured for the project.

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS. Access to our Firebase project is restricted to the app’s service accounts. Images you add to widgets or the gallery are stored as local caches on your device and synced alongside your content.

05Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following processors and embedded services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore) — account sign-in and cross-device sync. Google processes authentication data under the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase terms.
  • Google Sign-In — optional federated login; subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the app’s typefaces; requests may reveal your IP address to Google.
  • Spotify embed — the sidebar player is an embedded Spotify widget governed by the Spotify Privacy Policy.
  • Gemini API — only when you enable AI features; see the next section.

You can reduce third-party exposure by using the app offline: a signed-in session that has already loaded will continue to work from your device’s cache.

06AI Features

AI features are optional. When you enable them, the app sends the prompt and relevant context (for example, your profile notes and the tasks involved in your request) to the Google Gemini API using an API key that you supply. That means:

  • Content included in a prompt is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms.
  • You can review, edit or delete your profile notes at any time in AI settings.
  • If you never enable AI features, no data is sent to any AI provider.

07Your Rights & Choices

Wherever you live, we aim to give you practical control over your data:

  • Access & export: use Export All Data in the app footer to download a machine-readable copy of your content.
  • Correction: edit any task, goal, log or profile field directly in the app.
  • Local deletion: clearing your browser storage for this site removes locally cached data; see your browser’s documentation.
  • Cloud deletion & account closure: request deletion of your cloud-synced data and account by emailing us; we will remove the associated records within 30 days.
  • Objection: disable AI features or sign out and continue in guest mode, which processes everything locally.

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example the EU/EEA, the UK, or California), you may have additional statutory rights such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. To exercise them, contact us using the details below.

08Children’s Privacy

Havën Schedule is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this page, and the effective date at the top will be revised. Continued use of the service after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
