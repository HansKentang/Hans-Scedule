01Get in Touch

For any issue, question or feedback, email us and we will reply as soon as we can — typically within a few business days.

02Managing Your Data

Export

Use Export All Data in the app footer to download a JSON copy of your schedule, goals, finance and settings.

Import

Restore from a previous export on the same footer. Import replaces current data with the backup’s contents.

Local deletion

Clear site data via your browser settings to remove everything stored on this device.

Account deletion

Email us from your account address to delete your cloud-synced data and close the account.

03Frequently Asked Questions

Does Havën work offline?
Yes. It is an installable PWA: after the first visit, core pages are cached and everything keeps working offline. Changes made offline are stored on your device and sync when you are back online and signed in.
What is the difference between Guest and an account?
Guest mode keeps all data in the current browser only — nothing is uploaded. An account (Google or email) additionally syncs your data across devices through our Firebase backend. You can switch between them at any time from the sign-in page.
Why do AI features ask for an API key?
AI calls go directly from your browser to the Google Gemini API using your own key. This keeps the service free, means we never see or store your key, and gives you full control — you can revoke it anytime in Google AI Studio.
I lost my data after clearing browser data. Can you restore it?
If you were signed in with an account, sign in again and your data will sync down. If you were in guest mode, the data lived only in your browser and unfortunately cannot be recovered. Regular exports are strongly recommended for guests.
Which browsers are supported?
Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. The app is installable as a PWA on Chrome and Edge desktop and Android.
Is Havën free?
Yes — all current features are free. You only need your own (free-tier friendly) Gemini API key if you want the AI assistant.
How do I reset the Pomodoro timer or a stuck view?
Most UI state resets on reload. If something stays stuck, try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). Persistent issues can be fixed with Export → clear site data → Import.

04Before Writing In

  • Check this FAQ and the in-app guide (accessible from the hub’s FAB menu).
  • Try a hard refresh, and confirm whether the issue happens in another browser or private window.
  • If sync seems broken, verify you are signed in with the same account on both devices and that both are online.
